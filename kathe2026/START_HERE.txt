================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Problem: "HF_TOKEN not set"
Solution: set HF_TOKEN=your_token_here

Problem: "CUDA out of memory"
Solution: python finetune_improved.py --batch-size 1 --grad-accum 16

Problem: "Training too slow"
Solution: Remove --use-bpcc flag (faster but slightly lower score)

Problem: "Score still below 20"
Solutions:
  - Train longer: --epochs 10
  - More BPCC data: --bpcc-samples 10000
  - Higher LoRA rank: --lora-r 64 --lora-alpha 128
